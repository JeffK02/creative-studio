## WHAT TO PRODUCE

This account runs one copy per test, and the adset is flexible in size. So
produce three, for a hybrid setup:

### 1. Generic copy
Angle-neutral. It has to read naturally underneath any creative in the batch,
because it will sit under all of them. Built on the strongest proven angle, but
written so it does not contradict a creative pulling a different thread.

Long-form by default — problem, agitation, mechanism, proof, offer, close. Match
the length of the winning copy that already works.

### 2 and 3. Angle-matched copies
Pick the two strongest *new-angle* clusters from the selected concepts, and
write copy for each that matches that specific angle. These go in their own
adsets alongside the matched creatives, so congruence beats coverage here.

For each of the three, also give a short-form variant — three to five lines —
for cases where the angle is a single sharp claim that long-form would dilute.

## HOW TO WRITE IT

- Open on the wound, not on the product. The first line's only job is to earn
  the second.
- Each beat caused by the one before it. Not "and then, and then." The reader
  should slide.
- One emotion per copy. If a paragraph is pulling a different feeling than the
  rest, cut it.
- Where the buyer holds a limiting belief, agree with it first, then redirect.
  Never contradict a belief head-on — this buyer has been dismissed before, and
  arguing with her loses her at that line.
- Use her verbatim language from the brief. Her words beat clever ones.
- No claim that is not defensible from the product facts.

## OUTPUT

Markdown. For each of the three: a heading naming the angle and which concepts
it matches, then the long-form copy, then the short-form variant, then one line
on what it is testing.

End with a short note: which copy to pair with which creatives, and whether the
batch warrants a copy test at all or should run on the generic alone.
